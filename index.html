About Us

Welcome to Treekon (त्रिकोण). We are a deep tech startup building Quantum Diamond Magnetometers. Our mission is to commercialize high-precision instruments and bring laboratory proven quantum technologies into scalable, industrial environments.

Technology

Nitrogen-Vacancy Centers

We harness the quantum properties of NV centers in diamond to measure magnetic fields with extreme precision.

Room Temperature Operation

Operates at room temperature; Dramatically reducing overhead and deployment complexity.

Vector Magnetometry

Not just measurement of the magnitude of a magnetic field; A full directional vector data with high spatial resolution.

Applications

Defense & Security Intelligence

Detecting concealed armaments and underground weapon stockpiles. Ultra-sensitive magnetic vectoring allows for the non-invasive discovery of shielded ferrous materials, deep bunkers, and hidden caches.

GPS-Denied Navigation

Utilizing Earth's magnetic crustal anomalies to provide highly accurate, unjammable positioning and navigation data for aerospace and defense.

Ore & Subsurface Discovery

Mapping geological structures and detecting subterranean porosity. Identify valuable mineral deposits and subsurface voids by measuring minute magnetic anomalies.
